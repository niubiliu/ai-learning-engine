# AI Concept Explanation Template

This template defines the standard structure used to explain AI concepts.

When generating learning content, the system should organize the explanation using the following sections.

---

# 1 Concept Name

Provide the name of the AI concept being learned.

---

# 2 Concept Definition

Provide a concise definition of the concept.

---

# 3 Key Terminology

List important technical terms related to the concept and provide brief explanations.

---

# 4 Simple Example

Provide a simple and intuitive example to help understand the concept.

---

# 5 Application Scenarios

Describe situations where this concept is used in AI systems or products.

---

# 6 System Flow

Illustrate the basic workflow of how this concept operates within a system.

---

# 7 Component Structure

Show the structural relationship between the internal components or modules involved in this concept.

---

# 8 Technical Extensions

List related techniques or extensions associated with this concept.

---

# 9 Current Status

Describe the current development status of this technology, for example:

* widely adopted
* emerging technology
* experimental research
* gradually being replaced

---

# 10 Alternative Approaches

List alternative methods or related approaches.

---

# 11 Real-world Examples

Provide examples of real AI systems or products that use this concept.

---

# 12 References

List the sources used for the concept definition or technical description.

---

# 13 Knowledge Graph Nodes

Generate the knowledge graph structure for this concept, including:

Parent nodes
Child nodes
Related nodes
